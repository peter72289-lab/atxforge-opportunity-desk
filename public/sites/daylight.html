    Straight answers

    Before you get on a call with us.

    Are you an agency, a consultancy or a venture studio?
    A bit of each, which is why we call it a collective. Like an agency, we do the actual work: product, engineering, copy, creative, launch. Like a studio, we think in ventures and we'll take equity when it fits. Unlike either, there are four of us, all senior, and we're built for long relationships instead of one-off projects.
    We're an established company, not a startup. Still a fit?
    Yes, and that's a lot of our work. New product lines, new offers, new channels, internal tools, a rebuilt funnel. "New venture" doesn't have to mean "new company." It usually means a leader with a mandate and no free capacity.
    What does this cost?
    It depends on scope, and we'll give you a real number before you commit to anything. Sprints are fixed scope and fixed price. Embedded partnerships are a monthly rate. Venture deals trade part of the fee for equity or revenue share. No hourly billing, no surprise change orders.
    How fast will we see something?
    Faster than hiring, and you'll see working pieces every week rather than a reveal at the end. We'll commit to a real timeline once we've scoped the work, and we'd rather quote you something we can hit than something that sounds good on a website.
    Can you work with our existing team and vendors?
    Happily. We'll take a lane your team doesn't have capacity for, work alongside your current agency, or run point on the whole thing. We're there to add speed, not to start a turf war.
    Can we hire you for strategy only?
    Sometimes, if it's the honest first step. But it isn't where we're strongest. Our edge is that the people who make the plan also build it, and strategy without a build team is how ideas end up back on the list.
    Do we need to be in Austin?
    No. We're Austin-based and work with partners anywhere. If you're local, we'll buy the coffee and bring a whiteboard.
    What actually happens on the first call?
    Thirty minutes. You walk us through what's stuck, we ask the uncomfortable questions, and we tell you where we'd start and whether we're the right team. You leave with a clear next move either way.
