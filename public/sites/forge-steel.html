      Straight answers

      Before we talk.

      Are you an agency, a consultancy or a venture studio?
      Somewhere in between, on purpose. Like an agency, we do the actual work: build, copy, creative, launch. Like a studio, we think in ventures and we're comfortable taking equity when it makes sense. Unlike either, we're four senior partners with no bench of juniors, and we're built for long relationships instead of one-off projects.
      We're not a startup. Is this still for us?
      Absolutely. A lot of our best partners are established companies with a marketing leader or operator who has more good ideas than internal capacity. New product lines, new offers, new channels, internal tools: "new venture" doesn't have to mean "new company."
      How fast can we actually launch something?
      Most first launches go live in 4–8 weeks from kickoff. Some things, like a rebuilt offer and landing page, move in two. A full product takes longer, but you'll see working pieces every week, not a big reveal at the end.
      Do you take equity?
      Sometimes. Our Venture Partner model mixes a reduced fee with equity or revenue share when the founder, the idea and the distribution make sense. Most partnerships are fee-based. We'll tell you straight which one fits.
      Can you work alongside our existing team and vendors?
      Yes. We're happy to be the tip of the spear while your team keeps the core business running, or to slot in next to an existing agency and own a specific lane. Our job is to add speed, not start turf wars.
      Can we just hire you for strategy?
      We'll do a focused strategy engagement if it's the right first step, but that's not where we shine. Our edge is that the people who make the plan also execute it. Strategy without a build team is how ideas end up back on the list.
      You're in Austin. Do we need to be?
      No. We're rooted in Austin and work with partners everywhere. If you're local, we'll happily grab a coffee and a whiteboard.
      What happens on the first call?
      Thirty minutes. You walk us through the backlog, we pressure-test the idea most worth moving on, and you leave with a clear next step whether or not we work together. No slides, no pitch theater.
